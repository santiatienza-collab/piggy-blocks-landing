Why you'll love it

🎮

Two game modes

ARCADE: 100 levels, each starting with a fresh board of obstacles. MARATHON: up to 200 non-stop levels with backgrounds and colors that change every 10 levels.

🔥

Three difficulties

Easy to relax. Hard adds steel blocks and pink lines rising from the bottom. Impossible adds a piece limit per level.

🌍

World online ranking

Compete with players around the world. Sign in with Google or play as a guest, with separate leaderboards for each mode and difficulty.

✨

Super lines

Clear 4 lines at once and watch a piggy run across the screen to celebrate with a rainbow sweep.

🏅

Records & medals

Your personal record is always on the board, and you earn silent medals when you reach levels 10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150 and 200 in Marathon.
